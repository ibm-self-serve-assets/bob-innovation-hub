problem: >+
  - Business users often rely on raw Excel spreadsheets that are difficult to
  analyse and interpret.

  - Creating dashboards and KPI visualisations typically requires BI tools,
  technical expertise, and significant manual effort.

  - Decision-makers spend considerable time cleaning data, building reports, and
  identifying trends instead of acting on insights.

  - Business users cannot easily ask questions about their data in natural
  language and often depend on data analysts for answers.

  - Hidden patterns, anomalies, and business opportunities remain undiscovered
  due to limited analytical capabilities.

  - Traditional analytics solutions require multiple tools, lengthy
  implementation, and ongoing maintenance.

solution: >+
  - Users upload an Excel (.xlsx) spreadsheet containing business data.

  - IBM Bob automatically analyses the dataset and generates a complete
  analytics application.

  - The solution creates interactive dashboards, charts, KPI scorecards, and
  data visualisations with minimal user intervention.

  - IBM [watsonx.ai](http://watsonx.ai) enables users to interact with the data
  using natural language queries.

  - Users can explore trends, compare metrics, identify anomalies, and receive
  AI-generated insights and recommendations.

  - The generated application provides a self-service analytics experience
  without requiring coding or dashboard development skills.

  - The solution supports rapid data exploration and enables business users to
  make informed decisions from Excel data.

This asset transforms raw Excel spreadsheets into interactive dashboards, KPI visualisations, and actionable business insights using IBM Bob. By simply uploading an .xlsx file, users can automatically generate a complete analytics application and leverage IBM [watsonx.ai](http://watsonx.ai) to ask questions in natural language, explore trends, identify anomalies, and uncover data-driven recommendations—without writing code or configuring analytics tools
