problem: >-
  • Critical business logic resides in senior RPG developers and undocumented
  legacy applications, creating operational risk.  

  • Retirement of skilled IBM i developers is creating a growing knowledge and
  skills gap.  

  • Organizations need modern user experiences, APIs, and cloud integration
  while maintaining business continuity.  

  • Fixed-format RPG, green-screen applications, and Record Level Access (RLA)
  increase maintenance costs and limit agility.  

  • Traditional modernization efforts require significant manual analysis,
  documentation, and migration effort.
solution: >-
  • Fixed-format RPG → Free-format RPG conversion.  

  • Green Screen (5250) → Modern Web UI and API generation.  

  • RLA → Embedded SQL modernization and optimization.  

  • Direct IBM i connectivity for read, write, compile, test, and deploy.  

  • Pre-built IBM i skills for understanding, refactoring, documenting, testing,
  and transforming applications.
business-value: >-
  • Capture and document decades of business logic embedded within RPG, CL, DDS,
  and COBOL applications. • Accelerate onboarding and developer productivity.   

  • Modernize fixed-format RPG, green screens, and legacy data access patterns
  to support API-first and digital transformation initiatives.   

  • Lower modernization costs and timelines.   

  • Reduce modernization risk through AI-assisted analysis and testing.   

  • Shift team capacity from maintenance to innovation.
